Over the day you play 3 duels.

What matters most

The team with the most points in total across the 3 duels wins – not the team that wins the most duels.

Even when you are behind, every point still counts towards your total. So play to the last ball.

After each match

Drop by reception and tell us your result, for example "10 to 15". It appears on the screen straight away.
